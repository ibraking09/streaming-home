# Changelog

## v0.1.0

- Introduces a native Fire TV launcher with configurable favorites and an alphabetical All Apps grid.
- Uses installed app artwork, including TV banners and adaptive icons.
- Offers an optional scoped Home redirect.
- Preserves the Amazon Appstore and stock Home during intentional Amazon sessions.
- Adds guided Bash and PowerShell installers, manual instructions, and automated build and regression checks.
