# Roadmap

v0.1.0 covers the favorites row, All Apps, appearance controls, and optional Home redirection on the verified Fire TV configuration. The following are priorities for investigation and maintenance, with no release dates attached.

- **Broaden verified device coverage.** Collect reports for standalone installation and Home redirection separately. Record results by model and firmware and document reproducible limitations.
- **Establish a release performance baseline.** Document a repeatable procedure for PSS, direct startup, and Home-button redirection. Record the build, test conditions, sample counts, and timing boundaries.
- **Maintain the first-run experience.** Prioritize reproducible issues in remote focus, app discovery, and installation or recovery.

A [device compatibility report](https://github.com/ibraking09/streaming-home/issues/new?template=device_compatibility.yml) or a focused [bug report](https://github.com/ibraking09/streaming-home/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) helps identify where to start.
